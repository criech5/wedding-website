Best Things to See & Do

A short list of our favorites along the coast and beyond, in case you have extra time before or after the wedding.

Fast Facts About Bulgaria

🚗 Drive on the right side of the road, same as the US.
⛪ Orthodox Christianity is the main religion.
💶 The euro is now the official currency (Bulgaria switched over from the lev on January 1, 2026).
🗣️ Bulgarian is the official language, written in the Cyrillic alphabet - which Bulgaria actually created, back in the 9th century.
🛡️ Safety: we've always felt very safe here, at all hours.
🙂 A nod up-and-down can mean "no" and a side-to-side shake can mean "yes" here.
🕐 Bulgaria is on Eastern European Time (EET), 8 hours ahead of US Central Daylight Time.
🇪🇺 Bulgaria has been an EU member since 2007.

If You're Landing in Sofia

A couple of worthwhile stops if your flight routes through Sofia before the 4-hour drive to Sozopol.

History & Old Things

Rila Monastery

Bulgaria's largest and most famous monastery, close to Sofia - striped arches, frescoes, and mountain scenery all in one. If you love history and old buildings, this one's a must.

Full Day, Worth It

Seven Rila Lakes

A glacial lake hike in the Rila Mountains, day-trippable from Sofia. It's a full day commitment - cable car up, then a few hours on the trail past all seven lakes - but the views are incredible and worth the early start if you have a spare day near Sofia.

Maria's Favorite View

Shipka Mountain

Home to the Shipka Pass and its striking Freedom Monument, with sweeping views over the Balkan Mountains - one of Maria's favorite spots in the whole country.

Further Out

Plovdiv

One of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in Europe, with a beautifully preserved old town - a good stop if you're routing through Sofia.

If You're Landing in Burgas

Everything below is close to the coast, all within reach of Sozopol.

Old Town

Sozopol Old Town

Sozopol was founded in 610 BC. It's got lots of nightlife & charming houses, galleries, seafood tavernas, and sunset over the harbor.

Day Trip

Nessebar

A UNESCO World Heritage old town on its own small peninsula with roughly 3,000 years of history.

The Bride's Home City

Burgas & the Sea Garden

Maria's hometown and birthplace! Burgas is a lovely seaside city, and the Sea Garden is one of Cole and Maria's favorite local landmarks - open-air cafés, ice cream shops, beaches, plenty of benches for sitting, and tree canopies throughout the park that keep it shaded on hot days. It's about a 15-minute drive from Burgas airport.

Our Favorite

Arkutino Beach, Primorsko

Maria and Cole's favorite beach on the whole Black Sea coast - a wide, quieter stretch of sand near Primorsko, backed by dunes and pine forest. Well worth the short drive south of Sozopol.

Nature

Strandzha Nature Park

If you're after a hike near the area, Strandzha has a pretty waterfall and a cool and shady hike away from the beach.

Boat Trip

St. Anastasia Island

Bulgaria's only inhabited Black Sea island, just off the coast near Chernomorets, with a former monastery, a lighthouse, and a small museum - a great half-day escape. In summer, boats run from Burgas out to the island and back, making it an easy 2–3 hour round trip if you want a quick taste of the water without committing a full day.

Travel Info

Burgas Airport (BOJ) is closest to Sozopol and the best option if you can find a direct flight. Sofia (SOF) has more frequent service if you don't mind the extra drive.

Closest Airport

Burgas Airport - BOJ

About 40 minutes from Sozopol. Direct flights run seasonally (roughly May–September) from a good number of European cities. As of the most recent schedules, direct routes include:

🇬🇧 UK - London, Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Newcastle, Bristol 🇩🇪 Germany - several cities via Eurowings 🇦🇹 Austria - Vienna 🇵🇱 Poland - Warsaw, Kraków, Gdańsk, Katowice 🇮🇪 Ireland - Dublin 🇭🇺 Hungary - Budapest 🇮🇹 Italy - Rome 🇫🇷 France - Paris, Lille 🇸🇪 Sweden - Stockholm 🇳🇴 Norway - Oslo 🇩🇰 Denmark - Copenhagen 🇫🇮 Finland - Helsinki 🇱🇻 Latvia - Riga 🇨🇿 Czechia - Prague 🇸🇰 Slovakia - Bratislava, Košice 🇦🇪 UAE - Dubai

Routes and frequency shift year to year and by season, so double-check availability closer to July before booking.

Alternative

Sofia Airport - SOF

Bulgaria's main international hub, with far more frequent long-haul and connecting options - the better bet if you're coming from outside Europe or want more flight time flexibility.

Getting to Sozopol from Sofia: it's roughly a 4-hour drive (about 260 miles / 420 km) from Sofia Airport to Sozopol. Options include a rental car, a private transfer, or the bus/train to Burgas followed by a short taxi - but driving is by far the fastest.

Domestic

Flying Burgas ↔ Sofia

If your international flight only reaches Sofia, Bulgaria Air runs several domestic hops a day from Sofia to Burgas - and it's an easy one. It's only about a 45-minute flight, and Maria and Cole have flown this route themselves a number of times. Worth pricing against the 4-hour drive, especially if you're short on time.

Close to Bulgaria

Coming From Istanbul

Istanbul is closer than it looks on a map - about 300 km (185 miles) by road from Burgas, roughly a 5–6 hour bus ride depending on the border crossing. Several bus companies run direct routes between Istanbul and Burgas - a solid option if you're combining the wedding with a stop in Istanbul.

Wedding Day Inspiration

Bulgarian Food

A short field guide to what to order while you're in town.

Ш

Shopska Salad

Tomatoes, cucumber, peppers, onion, and a heavy grating of sirene (Bulgarian white cheese) on top. The default starter, and always a good one.

Б

Banitsa

Flaky pastry layered with egg and cheese - a breakfast staple, best eaten warm.

К

Kavarma

A slow-cooked clay-pot stew of meat, vegetables, and spices - rich, hearty, usually served bubbling.

К

Kebapche & Kyufte

Grilled minced-meat classics - kebapche is the elongated log, kyufte the flattened patty. Order both - one of Cole and Maria's favorites!

Т

Tarator

A cold summer soup of yogurt, cucumber, garlic, and dill - exactly what you want on a hot afternoon. Maria's favorite.

В

Pork Neck (Свинска Вратна)

Grilled or slow-roasted pork neck, tender and full of flavor - a menu staple, and the groom's favorite order.

Р

Rakia

Bulgaria's grape or fruit brandy, and the unofficial start to most meals. If someone offers you a glass - say yes.

С

Fresh Black Sea Fish

Ask the restaurant for the catch of the day!

М

Black Sea Mussels

Fresh-picked right from the sea! (DO NOT SKIP)

Ц

Tsatsa (Пържена цаца)

Bulgaria's popular little fried fish - deep-fried until crispy and served hot with fresh lemon and a cold beer. Cole was skeptical until he actually tried it - now it's one of his favorites.

К

Fries with Sirene

Fresh fries topped with shredded Bulgarian sirene (feta) - simple, salty, and always a good idea.

С

Chicken Soup

Almost every restaurant has it on the lunch menu, and it's always good.

Д

Bulgarian Tomatoes

Bulgarian tomatoes are the BEST IN THE WORLD!! Even if you don't like tomatoes, you will like these, we promise! There is something about the soil and how the tomatoes are grown that makes them so good!

Г

Fruit in general

Go to any local fruit stand, and buy whatever looks good. Fruit here is hard to beat. It all comes from local farms and villages. Recommendations: melon, peaches, and apricots!

★

Galaxy Ice Cream

A must-try, no debate - this is a shared favorite of both the bride and groom. Maria likes vanilla, and Cole likes chocolate.

Also worth trying

Bulgarian yogurt (genuinely different from what you're used to), sarmi (stuffed cabbage or vine leaves), and lyutenitsa (roasted pepper relish, usually on fresh bread).

There is SO MUCH good Bulgarian food. Our recommendation is to stay away from pizzas, pastas, burgers & "American" food. Stick to the classics - we know what we're good at and we do it well!
